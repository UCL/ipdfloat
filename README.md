# A Stata package to estimate within-trial interactions and covariate value-specific treatment effects using a two-stage IPD approach

In our paper Godolphin et al (Research Synthesis Methods, 2022) and with our Stata package **metafloat**,  we demonstrated a simple way of estimating pooled interactions and "floating" subgroup-specific treatment effects from a set of observed treatment effects by trial and by subgroup.  Here, we extend that approach to the individual participant data (IPD) setting, and generalise our methodology in a similar way as standard "two-stage" IPD meta-analysis pooling is to aggregate-data meta-analysis pooling.
